Business definition
The number of declined transaction attempts for a customer in the previous 24 hours, across all channels and merchants. A run of declines followed by a successful transaction is a textbook card-testing pattern.
Formula
SELECT customer_id, COUNT(*) AS customer_declined_txn_count_24h FROM TXN_STREAM WHERE txn_status = 'DECLINED' AND txn_ts BETWEEN CURRENT_TIMESTAMP - INTERVAL '24' HOUR AND CURRENT_TIMESTAMP GROUP BY customer_id;
